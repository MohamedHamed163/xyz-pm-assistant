# Software Development Proposal

Prepared for: Client / Business Owner · Prepared by: Project Management & Development Team · Version 1.0 (DPRP-DET)

## 1. Executive Summary

We propose building Droob AI in three delivery plans, each with its own scope, review and payment (DPRP-SUM).

<text color="success" weight="semibold">Total Project Investment: $3,000 USD (DPRP-COM1)</text>

## 2. Project Objectives

- Build an AI document learning platform with source-referenced answers.

## 3. Proposed Project Scope

Authentication, dashboard, PDF upload and processing, AI retrieval, chat, citations, QA and deployment.

## 4. Project Delivery Plans

Plan 2 — AI Integration & Intelligent Document Chat: chunking, embeddings, RAG and the chat interface (DPRP-SCP).

<text color="success" weight="semibold">Investment: $1,000 (DPRP-COM2)</text>

Acceptance criteria: users can ask questions and see source references where applicable.

## 5. Project Timeline

The project takes about 4–6 weeks; each plan takes 1–2 weeks (DPRP-TML).

## 6. Commercial Proposal

| Delivery Plan | Cost |
|---|---|
| Plan 1 | $1,000 |
| Plan 2 | $1,000 |
| Plan 3 | $1,000 |

Payment terms: each plan is billed separately and paid before it starts (DPRP-COM).

## 7. Project Management & Communication

Kickoff, regular progress updates and a milestone review at the end of each plan.

## 8. Client Responsibilities

Provide feedback and approvals within the agreed timelines (DPRP-ENG).

## 9. Exclusions

Third-party API fees, hosting and mobile applications are not included.

## 10. Warranty & Support

A 30-day post-delivery warranty covers defects in the agreed functionality.

## 11. Project Completion

Complete when all three plans are delivered, QA is done and handover documentation is delivered (DPRP-CMP).

## 12. Proposal Approval

Both parties sign to agree to scope, plans and commercial terms.
