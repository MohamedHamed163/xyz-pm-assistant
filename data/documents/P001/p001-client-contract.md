# Master Services Agreement — XYZ Software × Nile Retail Group

## 1. Parties <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

This agreement is between **XYZ Software LLC** ("Supplier") and **Nile Retail Group S.A.E.** ("Client"),
signed on 20 May 2026. Client signatory: Amina Fathy, Head of Digital.

## 2. Scope of Work <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

The Supplier will design, build and launch the NRG Online e-commerce platform as defined in the BRD
v1.3 (Sections 4 and 5), including 30 days of post-launch hypercare. Native mobile apps and the loyalty
programme are excluded and would require a separate statement of work.

## 3. Fees and Payment Terms <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

- **Total fixed fee: USD 486,000** (four hundred eighty-six thousand US dollars), excluding VAT.
- Payment schedule:

| Instalment | Trigger | Amount (USD) |
|------------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | Contract signature | 97,200 (20%) |
| 2 | Milestone M3 accepted | 121,500 (25%) |
| 3 | Milestone M5 (UAT) accepted | 145,800 (30%) |
| 4 | Go-live (M6) + 30 days hypercare | 121,500 (25%) |

- Invoices are payable within 30 days. Late payments accrue 1.5% per month.

## 4. Deliverables and Acceptance <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

Each milestone is delivered on staging. The Client has 10 working days to accept or list defects.
Silence after 10 working days counts as acceptance.

## 5. Penalties and Service Levels <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

- Delay penalty: 0.5% of the total fee per full week of delay to go-live caused by the Supplier,
  capped at 5%.
- Post-launch availability target: 99.5% monthly; service credits of 2% of the monthly support fee per
  0.1% below target.

## 6. Confidentiality and IP <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

Source code and IP transfer to the Client upon final payment. Both parties keep each other's information
confidential for 3 years after termination.

## 7. Internal Negotiation Notes <!-- visibility: management -->

*Internal — XYZ Software only. Never share with the client.*

- Our internal cost estimate is USD 352,000, giving a target gross margin of 27.6%.
- Walk-away price during negotiation was USD 440,000. NRG's first counter-offer was USD 410,000.
- We conceded the 10-day silent-acceptance clause in exchange for dropping a 10% delay-penalty cap.
- The Odoo connector risk (BRD R-1) is not priced in; plan to raise it as a change request (~USD 38,000).
