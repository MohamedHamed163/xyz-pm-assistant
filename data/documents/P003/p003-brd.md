# Hub Drives — Business Requirements Document

## 1. Purpose <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

Hub Drives Mobility LLC wants a peer-to-peer car rental marketplace in the UAE: private car owners list
their cars, verified renters book them by the day, and Hub Drives takes a commission on each trip.

## 2. Business Objectives <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

- BO-1: 1,500 listed cars in Dubai and Abu Dhabi within 9 months of launch.
- BO-2: Average 11 booked days per car per month.
- BO-3: Damage-claim resolution in under 72 hours.
- BO-4: Commission of 20% per trip, with insurance bundled.

## 3. Scope <!-- visibility: client_shared -->

Owner listing and pricing, renter identity and licence verification (UAE Pass + Emirates ID scan),
booking and calendar, deposits and payments, trip check-in/check-out with photos, damage claims, ratings.
